🧪Initial Diagnostic Workup (NCCN MYEL-1)

Laboratory

  • CBC with differential
  • Peripheral smear / flow for circulating plasma cells
  • BUN, creatinine, electrolytes, LFTs, albumin
  • Calcium, uric acid, LDH, β2-microglobulin
  • Creatinine clearance (24-h or calculated)
  • Serum quantitative immunoglobulins, SPEP, SIFE
  • 24-h urine: total protein, UPEP, UIFE
  • Serum free light chain assay (κ/λ, ratio)
  • NT-proBNP (amyloid screening)

Imaging

  • Whole-body FDG-PET/CT — preferred
  • Low-dose whole-body CT — alternative
  • Whole-body MRI if PET/CT negative, to distinguish smoldering from active disease
  • Dedicated MRI for suspected cord compression

Marrow & genetics

  • Unilateral aspirate + biopsy with IHC and/or flow cytometry
  • Plasma-cell FISH: del(13q), del(17p13), t(4;14), t(11;14), t(14;16), t(14;20), 1q21 gain/amp, 1p deletion
  • NGS for TP53 mutation
  • Consider baseline clonotype for future MRD testing

🛡️Toxicity & Prophylaxis Management

🧰Supportive Care — All Patients

🦴 Bone health

  • Zoledronic acid 4 mg IV every 4 weeks (or every 12 weeks after 1 year if stable) — first-line for all with bone disease.
  • Denosumab 120 mg SC every 4 weeks — preferred if CrCl <30 mL/min or on dialysis.
  • Calcium 500–1000 mg + vitamin D 400–800 IU daily.
  • Dental examination before starting; osteonecrosis of the jaw risk with both agents.
  • No routine radiotherapy; use for painful focal lesions or cord compression.

🩸 Venous thromboembolism

  • Risk-stratify with IMPEDE-VTE or SAVED score.
  • Low risk: aspirin 81–325 mg daily.
  • High risk (IMiD + high-dose dex, prior VTE, obesity, central line, cardiac disease, immobility): LMWH or DOAC (apixaban 2.5 mg BID or rivaroxaban 10 mg daily).
  • Continue prophylaxis throughout IMiD therapy; reassess at each cycle.

🦠 Infection prophylaxis

  • Herpes zoster: acyclovir 400 mg BID or valacyclovir 500 mg daily for all on proteasome inhibitors or anti-CD38.
  • PJP: TMP-SMX single-strength daily (or dapsone / atovaquone if sulfa-allergic) for patients on ≥20 mg prednisone-equivalent daily for >4 weeks, or on anti-CD38.
  • Antibacterial: consider levofloxacin 500 mg daily during the first 2–3 months of bortezomib-based therapy.
  • IVIG 0.4 g/kg every 4 weeks if IgG <400 mg/dL (<4 g/L) with recurrent infections.
  • HBV: screen HBsAg, anti-HBc, anti-HBs before anti-CD38; antiviral prophylaxis if positive.
  • Vaccines: influenza annually, pneumococcal, COVID-19, RSV, recombinant zoster. Avoid live vaccines.

🫘 Renal & metabolic

  • Avoid NSAIDs, iodinated contrast, aminoglycosides.
  • Maintain hydration ≥2 L/day if tolerated; alkalinise urine if light-chain burden high.
  • Tumour lysis: allopurinol 300 mg daily (dose-adjust in renal impairment) or rasburicase; monitor uric acid, K⁺, PO₄³⁻, Ca²⁺.
  • Plasmapheresis for symptomatic hyperviscosity or acute cast nephropathy with high FLC.

💉 Transplant-specific

  • Mobilisation: G-CSF 10 mcg/kg SC daily ± plerixafor; target ≥2 × 10⁶ CD34⁺/kg (≥4–6 × 10⁶ for tandem).
  • Hold lenalidomide ≥2 weeks before apheresis (some protocols 4 weeks).
  • Melphalan 200 mg/m² IV day −2; reduce to 140 mg/m² if CrCl <40 mL/min, age >70, or significant comorbidity.
  • Post-transplant: neutropenic fever precautions, mucositis care, engraftment monitoring, revaccination at 6–12 months.
